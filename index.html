The truth: Your thumbnail is the single biggest lever for clicks. A great video with a bad thumbnail will underperform every time. Your face, one emotion, five words max. Design in Canva. Always preview at small size before uploading.
🖼️ The Golden Rules
👤
Your Face Always
Emotion drives clicks. Your face should take up at least 40% of the thumbnail.
📝
5 Words Max
Bold, high contrast, readable on a phone screen. More than 5 words — cut it.
😮
One Emotion
Curiosity, shock, joy, drama or sass. Don't tell the whole story — just make them click.
🎨
High Contrast
Your thumbnail competes with thousands. Bright colors, strong contrast.
📱
Preview Small
Most people find you on phones. Check your thumbnail at thumbnail size first.
🎨
Use Canva
Free, easy, YouTube templates built in. Use Background Remover to cut yourself out cleanly.

📖 Tell the Story, Don't Just Document
As Told By Yolanda is a storytelling channel. The best vloggers don't just film their day — they frame it as a chapter. Every video should feel like it belongs to a larger story. What chapter is this? What came before? What comes next? When viewers feel like they're following a story, they become invested and they come back.
⏰ Consistency Is Everything
Tuesday and Thursday uploads, every week. Going silent costs momentum that takes weeks to rebuild. Batch record — film 3–4 videos in one session so you always have a buffer. Always have 2–3 videos ready to go before you need them.
🎬 The First 30 Seconds Are Everything
YouTube measures audience retention heavily. If people leave in the first 30 seconds YouTube stops pushing your video. Don't do long intros — just START. Drop them into the most interesting moment first, then pull back.
🖼️ Thumbnail + Title = 90% of Your Clicks
Before you edit a single frame, decide your thumbnail and title. If you can't make a compelling thumbnail from this content, film something specifically for the thumbnail. The thumbnail is not an afterthought. It's the first edit.
🇯🇲 Your Culture Is Your Algorithm Advantage
There are millions of lifestyle vloggers. There are very few Jamaican immigrant women telling their story with this level of honesty and humor. Lean into your specificity — the more specifically Jamaican, the more universally it resonates. Niche is not a limitation, it's a superpower.
📊 Watch Time Over Everything
YouTube's most important metric is average view duration. Check your analytics weekly. Find the timestamps where people drop off and ask why. Keep your videos moving — every minute should earn the next one.
🔁 Build Series That Make People Binge
Your numbered vlogs (V1, V2, V3…) are already a series. Reference past chapters, tease future ones. Series create loyal subscribers who feel like they're watching a show. Consider dedicated series: Korea Throwbacks, Sunday Dinners with Mom, Weight Loss Journal Updates.
🌍 You're Building a Legacy, Not Just a Following
As Told By Yolanda is a record of a life being lived fully and honestly. Every video you make is a chapter that will exist forever. Post as if you're building an archive — someday people will binge from V1 to V100 and watch you become who you're becoming. That's the real mission. 🤍🇯🇲
