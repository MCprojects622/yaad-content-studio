🎥 Visual Hooks — When You're Not Talking

🎬

🔍

✍️
Bold text overlay on screen that makes them read before they scroll — then the video delivers on it
😮

✂️
A satisfying or unexpected visual transition that makes them rewatch
🌀
Start in the middle of chaos — something is already happening, they have to catch up

🗺️ Your Platform Ecosystem
TikTok finds your people. Instagram keeps them. YouTube is where they fall in love with you. Everything works together — one brand, three depths.
🎵 TikTok
Your Growth Engine — Post Here First, Post Here Most
2–3× more reach for new creators
Zero followers = viral potential
Interest-based algorithm
4–5× per week
  • ✅
    The algorithm is purely interest-based — follower count barely matters. A new account can go viral overnight if the content holds attention.
  • ⏱️
    Watch time and completion rate are everything. Make videos people watch all the way through or replay. Shorter (under 60 sec) usually wins.
  • 💬
    Engage in the first 60 minutes after posting — posts without early engagement get capped. Reply to every comment when you first post.
  • 🎯
    Stay in your niche. Posting across 3+ unrelated topics cuts your reach by 45%. Your pillars are cohesive enough — lean in.
  • 🎵
    Check trending audio weekly — especially dancehall and afrobeats. Adding trending sound extends your reach even on non-talking videos.
  • 🚫
    Don't go silent for more than a week — recovery takes 2–12 weeks. Batch record so you always have videos ready.
⚡ Always export the original clean file to post on both platforms. Never download from TikTok and repost to Instagram — the watermark kills your reach by 30–50%.
📸 Instagram
Your Community & Conversion Platform
Reels: 55% non-follower views
DM shares = strongest signal
Keywords > Hashtags
3× per week Reels
  • 📤
    DM shares are the most powerful signal on Instagram right now. Content that makes people send it to a friend gets pushed harder. Your joy and healing content is built for this.
  • 🔍
    Keywords in captions matter more than hashtags now. Write captions with words people would actually search — "journaling prompts", "self belief", "Jamaican culture".
  • 📱
    Reels under 90 seconds get pushed to non-followers. Keep it tight. 30–90 seconds is the sweet spot for reach.
  • ✨
    Unpolished and authentic consistently outperforms heavily produced. Your chaos aesthetic is an advantage, not a liability.
  • 🔁
    Carousels get a "second chance" — if someone scrolls past, Instagram may show it again from slide 2. Great for healing/mindset content people want to save.

Be It, Don't Just Talk About It
You're a reverse influencer — which means you don't lecture, you live it on camera. The getting ready while singing, the Friday night in with LOTR, the necklace struggle with acrylics. That IS the brand. Capture what you already do. The content is your life.
🪞
Real Over Polished — Always
Unpolished, authentic content consistently outperforms heavily produced videos. Good lighting and clear audio are your only non-negotiables. Natural light from a window is enough. Your chaos aesthetic is an advantage — people are exhausted by perfection.
📦
Batch + Capture Strategy
Work in two modes. Capture mode — always on, phone ready, film life as it happens. Batch mode — once a week, 30–45 minutes, record 3–5 intentional videos. Goal: always have 7–10 videos queued so you never scramble or go silent.
⏰
Consistency Is the Only Algorithm Hack
TikTok: 4–5× per week. Instagram Reels: 3× per week. Going silent for a week costs 2–12 weeks of recovery. Build your buffer first — record 10 videos before you start posting consistently. Consistency beats virality every time.
💬
The First 60 Minutes Matter
After you post, stay active. Reply to every comment in the first hour. The algorithm reads early engagement as a signal to push your video further. Don't post and disappear.
🔁
Build Recurring Formats
Formats build habit. When your audience knows what to expect — "Songs That Changed My Life", "Jamaican Girl Reacts", "Friday Night In" — they come back looking for it. Pick 2–3 recurring formats and run them consistently.
🇯🇲
Your Culture Is Your Superpower
Your Jamaican identity — the accent, the humour, the perspective — is your biggest differentiator. Nobody else has your combination. Use it as the lens for everything, not just the culture-specific videos. It belongs in every pillar.
📊
Check Analytics Weekly
Look at watch time and completion rate above everything else. When something works, make more of that. When something dies, learn from it and move on. The data will tell you what your audience loves faster than any strategy document will.
🎵
Trending Audio Weekly
Check TikTok's trending sounds every week — especially dancehall, afrobeats, and R&B for your niche. Adding trending audio to Reels and even photo posts extends your reach into the Reels feed. Don't force it — only use audio that actually fits.
🌍
You're Building a Movement, Not Just a Following
The reverse influencer vision is what makes you different from everyone else. Every video should leave someone feeling something — lighter, more seen, more inspired, more themselves. That's the mission. Stay connected to it when the algorithm feels discouraging.
2026 reality: Hashtags no longer drive discovery the way they used to. On Instagram, keywords in your captions now matter more. On TikTok, speech recognition reads your content directly. Use 5 hashtags max — choose them for categorisation, not as your growth strategy.
